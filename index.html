GOOD TO KNOW

to know.

Everything you need to feel right at home before you arrive.

What time is check-in and check-out?+

Check-in begins at 2:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM. Need a little flexibility? Let our front desk know.

Can I change or cancel my booking?+

Free cancellation is available up to 48 hours before check-in. Please review your booking confirmation for the details of your rate.

Is breakfast included in my stay?+

Yes, breakfast is included with every room rate and is served until noon.

Do you welcome pets?+

Small pets are welcome in Classic and Deluxe rooms for an additional $15 per day.
